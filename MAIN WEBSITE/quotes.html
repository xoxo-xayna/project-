MINI LESSONS

Tap and read. 2 minutes each.

1. ROCKETS: Why fire goes down to go up

Think of a balloon. When you let it go, air rushes out one way, balloon flies the other way. Rockets do the same but with fire. They push fire down, so they go UP. That's it. No magic.

2. PLANES: The secret of the wing

Look at a bird's wing. Top is curved, bottom is flat. Air moves faster on top. Faster air = less pressure. So air pushes the wing UP. Engineers copied birds. That's LIFT.

3. SPACE: Why astronauts float

There IS gravity in space. The ISS is just falling around Earth at 17,500 mph and missing it. Because they are falling together, everything inside floats. We call it "zero-g".

4. NIGERIA: We are already in space

NASRDA launched NigeriaSat-1 and NigeriaSat-2. They watch our farms, floods, and weather. The future jobs are here: drone pilots, satellite engineers, aerospace. The future is now.

5. DRONES: Baby planes

A drone uses the same science as a plane, but instead of 1 big wing, it uses 4 small spinning wings called propellers. Same push, smaller size.
