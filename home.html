Fact That Will Blow Your Mind

1. Rockets carry their own oxygen. That's why they can burn fire even in space where is no air.

2. Satellities are falling.They move at 17,500 mph and keep missing the Earth. That's what we call "orbit"

3. Planes don't beat gravity. They outsmart it. The shape of the wing pushes air down, so the plane goes up.
